Aristocrats
Back to SearchBest Aristocrats Commanders for EDH
Aristocrats decks are for players who enjoy turning expendable creatures into cards, Treasure, damage, and life drain whenever they die. This strategy wants to make cheap bodies, use sacrifice commanders and free outlets, and convert each death trigger into value to win through drain effects or recursive sacrifice loops. Unlike Tokens, it cares less about attacking with many bodies, and unlike broad Sacrifice decks it is specifically built around death trigger payoffs. The best aristocrats commanders let you grind from small pieces while the table decides whether removing your board helps you or hurts them.
Best Colors for Aristocrats Decks
Orzhov supplies tokens, recursion, black drain payoffs, and sacrifice outlets for classic aristocrats decks.
Rakdos pairs sacrifice payoffs with damage triggers and Treasure to pressure life totals quickly.
Mardu combines white tokens, black drain, and red damage for dense outlet and payoff packages.
Golgari adds recursion and creature value, letting the same bodies die more than once.
How Do Aristocrats Decks Win in Commander?
Aristocrats decks excel through a specific playstyle and win conditions. Here's how they work:
✓ You deploy cheap creatures and token makers that are useful when they die.
✓ You set up free sacrifice outlets so bodies become value at instant speed.
✓ You stack death trigger payoffs that drain life draw cards or make Treasure.
✓ You rebuild after sweepers with low cost pieces and recursion.
✓ You close by chaining sacrifices until repeated drain finishes the table.
Best Cards for Aristocrats EDH Decks
Blood Artist
Signature death trigger payoff that drains an opponent whenever creatures die.
Zulaport Cutthroat
Drains each opponent when your creatures die, scaling well in multiplayer.
Viscera Seer
Free sacrifice outlet that scries and lets you respond to removal.
Pitiless Plunderer
Converts creature deaths into Treasure to fund longer sacrifice chains.
Mayhem Devil
Turns sacrificed permanents into damage, adding reach and spot removal.
How to Build a Aristocrats Commander Deck
Core Components
✓Run 8-10 death trigger payoffs like Blood Artist, Zulaport Cutthroat, and Bastion of Remembrance
✓Include 5-8 free sacrifice outlets such as Viscera Seer, Carrion Feeder, and Ashnod's Altar
✓Pack 10-12 fodder sources, using cheap recursive creatures and token makers to keep outlets active
✓Add recursion so key payoffs and outlets return after removal or sweepers
Deck Structure
✓Keep the mana curve low so most engine pieces cost one to three mana
✓Favor creatures that replace themselves or create extra bodies when they enter or die
✓Include sacrifice-based removal that doubles as interaction and trigger fuel
✓Use Treasure producers like Pitiless Plunderer to turn deaths into mana
Win Conditions
✓Drain the table with stacked death triggers during a mass sacrifice turn
✓Assemble loops that repeatedly sacrifice and return a creature for infinite triggers
✓Grind opponents out of resources, then finish with repeated drain
Weaknesses of Aristocrats Decks
⚠Aristocrats is vulnerable to graveyard hate like Rest in Peace, which shuts off recursion and some death loops.
⚠The deck can struggle against exile removal or effects that stop death triggers from resolving.
⚠Tax effects on activated abilities can slow free outlets enough for opponents to stabilize.
⚠Drain damage often accumulates over several turns, so fast combo can win before the engine matters.
Is Aristocrats Right for You?
Aristocrats fits players who enjoy small decisions, sacrifice timing, and turning removal into another trigger. The skill floor is moderate because you must track death triggers, but the core loop of fodder, outlet, and payoff is clear. Budget builds are realistic because many staple outlets and drain payoffs are inexpensive.
Related Commander Strategies
Sacrifice
Sacrifice is the broader engine; Aristocrats stacks death trigger payoffs as the win route.
Tokens
Tokens makes many bodies to use; Aristocrats sacrifices those bodies for death triggers.
Lifegain
Life drain overlaps, but Lifegain starts from life triggers instead of creature deaths.
Graveyard
Aristocrats fills the graveyard incidentally; Graveyard decks make recursion the central engine.
