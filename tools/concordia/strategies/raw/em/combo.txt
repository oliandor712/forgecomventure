Combo
Back to SearchBest Combo Commanders for EDH
Combo decks are for players who enjoy finding a narrow line, protecting it, and ending the game with specific cards working together. This strategy wants to assemble combo commanders, tutors, draw, and compact two-card or three-card packages, using infinite combos or deterministic loops, to win in one decisive moment. Unlike Spellslinger it is not paid for every spell cast, unlike Storm it does not need a long chain turn, and unlike Big Mana it does not rely on casting one enormous payoff. The best combo decks EDH players build balance speed with enough interaction to survive the table's disruption.
Best Colors for Combo Decks
Dimir offers tutors, counterspells, and compact wins like Thassa's Oracle lines.
Izzet supports copy loops and Dramatic Scepter mana with blue protection.
Orzhov uses lifegain loops, enchantment tutors, and Silence effects to protect wins.
Golgari builds creature loops with persist, undying, sacrifice outlets, and death triggers.
How Do Combo Decks Win in Commander?
Combo decks excel through a specific playstyle and win conditions. Here's how they work:
✓ You build around a small number of card combinations that win once assembled.
✓ You spend early turns ramping drawing and tutoring toward those pieces.
✓ You hold protection to push the combo through disruption.
✓ You read the table before committing because a failed attempt changes how opponents treat you.
✓ You win in one decisive moment rather than through combat pressure.
Best Cards for Combo EDH Decks
Demonic Tutor
Finds the missing piece that turns a partial setup into a win attempt.
Thassa's Oracle
Wins with an empty library after Consultation-style or self-mill setups.
Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker
Combines with untappers to create unlimited hasty attackers.
Isochron Scepter
Pairs with Dramatic Reversal and mana rocks to produce unlimited mana.
Exquisite Blood
Forms an infinite drain loop with Sanguine Bond.
How to Build a Combo Commander Deck
Core Components
✓Choose 2-3 compact combos that share colors or pieces, such as Exquisite Blood lines
✓Run 5-10 tutors such as Demonic Tutor, Mystical Tutor, and Enlightened Tutor
✓Include 8-12 card draw and selection effects to dig when tutors run out
✓Add a clear outlet for infinite mana or triggers, such as Walking Ballista
Protection & Interaction
✓Reserve 6-10 slots for counterspells, Silence effects, or protection pieces
✓Use cheap removal to buy time and break up opposing combo attempts
✓Play redundant pieces because one removal spell can stop a narrow line
Win Conditions
✓Turn infinite mana into an X spell or activated ability that ends the game
✓Use Kiki-Jiki style loops for unlimited attackers or repeated death triggers
✓Use Thassa's Oracle packages when the deck wants a compact noncombat finish
Weaknesses of Combo Decks
⚠A single counterspell or removal spell at the wrong moment can stop the whole plan without backup pieces.
⚠Once the table knows your combo, opponents often save interaction for you first.
⚠Stax pieces and tax effects can slow tutors and spells enough to leave the deck stranded.
⚠Tutor-heavy builds can feel repetitive if they find the same line every game.
Is Combo Right for You?
Combo suits players who enjoy deckbuilding puzzles, sequencing, and choosing the right window for a win attempt. Power level varies widely, so match your combo speed and tutor density to the table before the game starts. Budget entry points exist, but fast tutor-heavy builds get expensive quickly.

Frequently Asked Questions
What makes a good Combo commander?
The best combo commanders typically provide consistent access to the strategy's core mechanics. Look for commanders that you build around a small number of card combinations that win once assembled.
How do Combo decks win?
Combo decks win through a combination of Demonic Tutor, Thassa's Oracle, Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker, and other synergistic pieces that combo decks are for players who enjoy finding a narrow line, protecting it, and ending the game with specific cards working together.
What are the best colors for Combo?
Dimir offers tutors, counterspells, and compact wins like Thassa's Oracle lines. However, Izzet supports copy loops and Dramatic Scepter mana with blue protection.
What are the weaknesses of Combo decks?
A single counterspell or removal spell at the wrong moment can stop the whole plan without backup pieces. Once the table knows your combo, opponents often save interaction for you first. Stax pieces and tax effects can slow tutors and spells enough to leave the deck stranded. Tutor-heavy builds can feel repetitive if they find the same line every game.
