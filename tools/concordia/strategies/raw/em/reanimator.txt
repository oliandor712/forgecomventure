Reanimator
Back to SearchBest Reanimator Commanders for EDH
Reanimator decks are for players who want to turn the graveyard into a shortcut for creatures that should cost far more mana. This strategy wants to load its own graveyard with high-impact targets, using discard, self-mill, and Entomb-style tutors, to win by casting cheap reanimation spells that cheat creatures into play ahead of schedule. Unlike Graveyard value decks, Reanimator is less about recurring small resources every turn, and unlike Mill it is not trying to empty libraries as the primary weapon. The best reanimator commanders and graveyard commanders help discard threats, return them, or convert each reanimated creature into immediate pressure.
Best Colors for Reanimator Decks
Mono-black supplies the cheapest reanimation spells, discard outlets, tutors, and life-for-mana exchanges.
Dimir adds blue self-mill, card selection, and counterspells to protect the reanimation turn.
Orzhov pairs black recursion with white protection, removal, and resilient creature returns.
Golgari adds green self-mill, creature tutors, and regrowth effects that reuse spent recursion.
How Do Reanimator Decks Win in Commander?
Reanimator decks excel through a specific playstyle and win conditions. Here's how they work:
✓ You put expensive creatures into your graveyard before you could cast them normally.
✓ You use discard self-mill and tutors to find the right target for the table.
✓ You cast cheap reanimation spells that convert the graveyard into battlefield pressure.
✓ You choose creatures with enter triggers attack triggers or static effects that matter right away.
✓ You hold backup recursion and answers for graveyard hate before committing.
✓ You close with repeated reanimation or one mass return spell.
Best Cards for Reanimator EDH Decks
Reanimate
Trades life for mana efficiency by returning the best creature in any graveyard.
Animate Dead
Low-cost aura recursion that brings back a creature and supports repeatable sacrifice loops.
Entomb
Puts the exact creature you need into the graveyard at instant speed.
Buried Alive
Stocks three creature targets at once for single-target or mass reanimation turns.
Living Death
Turns stocked graveyards into a new battlefield while clearing creatures already in play.
How to Build a Reanimator Commander Deck
Core Components
✓Run 8-12 reanimation spells such as Reanimate, Animate Dead, Necromancy, and Victimize
✓Include 6-8 graveyard setup cards like Entomb, Buried Alive, looting spells, and self-mill
✓Choose 6-10 targets with immediate impact, such as Archon of Cruelty or Ashen Rider
✓Add cheap creatures that block early and give Victimize or sacrifice outlets usable fuel
Deck Structure
✓Keep setup and recursion costs low so your first threat arrives before normal casting curves
✓Favor enter-the-battlefield triggers because removal cannot undo the value already gained
✓Reserve interaction for graveyard hate, exile removal, and counterspells on the reanimation spell
✓Mix targets between attackers, engines, and answer creatures so the graveyard remains flexible
Win Conditions
✓Reanimate an early threat that attacks resources, life totals, or opposing boards immediately
✓Use Living Death style effects when your graveyard contains several creatures worth returning
✓Loop creatures with sacrifice outlets and reanimation when one large target cannot finish alone
Weaknesses of Reanimator Decks
⚠Graveyard hate like Rest in Peace or Bojuka Bog can remove every stocked target at once.
⚠Instant-speed exile can answer the reanimated creature after you spend both setup and recursion.
⚠Hands with reanimation but no target, or targets with no discard outlet, can stall badly.
⚠The key turn is easy to read, so opponents often save interaction for the first recursion spell.
Is Reanimator Right for You?
Reanimator has a clear loop: move a creature to the graveyard, then return it for less mana than it should cost. It suits players who enjoy planning one strong turn and can accept that graveyard hate sometimes resets the work. Budget builds can use cheaper targets and recursion, while premium tutors and namesake spells raise the ceiling.
Related Commander Strategies
Sacrifice
Sacrifice spends creatures for value; Reanimator uses the graveyard to return larger threats.
Graveyard
Graveyard decks recur many resources; Reanimator cheats high-impact creatures into play.
Mill
Mill attacks libraries or fuels engines; Reanimator stocks its own graveyard with targets.
