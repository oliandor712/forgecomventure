Enchantress
Back to SearchBest Enchantress Commanders for EDH
Enchantress decks are for players who want each enchantment cast to replace itself and add another layer to the board. This strategy wants to chain cheap enchantments, using enchantress commanders, draw engines, and protective pieces, to win through constellation triggers, token makers, or an aura-based Voltron line. Unlike general Card Draw decks, Enchantress ties its cards to enchantment casts, and unlike Lifegain it treats life from Sythis-style triggers as support rather than the main engine. The best enchantment decks EDH players build feel incremental: ramp a little, draw a card, protect the engine, then convert the chain into threats.
Best Colors for Enchantress Decks
Selesnya supplies classic enchantress draw, aura ramp, protection, and enchantment removal.
Bant adds blue counterspells, extra draw, and aura tools to protect the enchantment engine.
Orzhov trades green draw creatures for removal enchantments, drain, and Daxos-style token payoffs.
How Do Enchantress Decks Win in Commander?
Enchantress decks excel through a specific playstyle and win conditions. Here's how they work:
✓ You cast cheap enchantments that replace themselves through enchantress triggers.
✓ You stack two or more draw engines so each spell keeps the chain moving.
✓ You protect the engine with shroud effects recursion and enchantment-based removal.
✓ You convert repeated casts into tokens counters life or larger attackers.
✓ You finish with constellation payoffs an aura Voltron threat or a sustained card lead.
Best Cards for Enchantress EDH Decks
Argothian Enchantress
Cheap protected draw engine that rewards every enchantment you cast.
Enchantress's Presence
Enchantress draw on an enchantment, making the engine harder to remove.
Sythis, Harvest's Hand
Puts card draw and lifegain in the command zone for each enchantment cast.
Sterling Grove
Protects your enchantments and tutors the missing engine piece when needed.
Sigil of the Empty Throne
Converts later enchantment casts into Angel tokens that close through combat.
How to Build a Enchantress Commander Deck
Core Components
✓Run 6-10 enchantress effects such as Argothian Enchantress, Enchantress's Presence, Setessan Champion, and Mesa Enchantress
✓Fill the curve with cheap enchantments like Wild Growth, Utopia Sprawl, and removal auras
✓Include protection such as Sterling Grove, Greater Auramancy, and Heroic Intervention
✓Add recursion like Hall of Heliod's Generosity to rebuild after sweepers
Deck Structure
✓Keep the average mana value low so one- and two-mana enchantments trigger more draws
✓Use enchantment ramp like Sanctum Weaver, Wild Growth, and Fertile Ground as acceleration and triggers
✓Play 34-36 lands because aura ramp and steady draw smooth land drops
✓Favor enchantment removal like Grasp of Fate and Darksteel Mutation over spells that do not trigger engines
Win Conditions
✓Use Sigil of the Empty Throne or Ajani's Chosen to turn casts into tokens
✓Grow Setessan Champion or constellation payoffs as a natural result of playing enchantments
✓Stack auras on one protected creature when an aura Voltron line is open
✓Win longer games by drawing more cards than opponents can trade for
Weaknesses of Enchantress Decks
⚠Killing early enchantress creatures can leave you casting enchantments without drawing cards.
⚠Mass enchantment removal like Farewell or Bane of Progress can undo several turns of setup.
⚠The engine often needs time to assemble, which leaves openings for fast combo or combat decks.
⚠Hands full of payoffs but few cheap enchantments can stall before the chain starts.
Is Enchantress Right for You?
Enchantress is a friendly engine deck because the core loop is simple: cast an enchantment, draw a card, keep going. It suits players who enjoy building a protected board over several turns rather than racing combat early. Sythis keeps budget builds functional, while premium pieces mostly add speed and resilience.
Related Commander Strategies
Card Draw
Card Draw is broader; Enchantress draws specifically from casting enchantments.
Voltron
Voltron can use Auras; Enchantress treats aura attacks as one payoff line.
Lifegain
Lifegain makes life the engine; Enchantress uses life as support for enchantment chains.

Frequently Asked Questions
What makes a good Enchantress commander?
The best enchantress commanders typically provide consistent access to the strategy's core mechanics. Look for commanders that you cast cheap enchantments that replace themselves through enchantress triggers.
How do Enchantress decks win?
Enchantress decks win through a combination of Argothian Enchantress, Enchantress's Presence, Sythis, Harvest's Hand, and other synergistic pieces that enchantress decks are for players who want each enchantment cast to replace itself and add another layer to the board.
What are the best colors for Enchantress?
