Tokens
Back to SearchBest Tokens Commanders for EDH
Token decks are for players who want one card to become many bodies and then decide whether those bodies attack, block, draw cards, or fuel another engine. This strategy wants to flood the battlefield with creatures, using token commanders, repeatable generators, and anthem effects, to win through a go wide attack or by converting the swarm into damage, drain, or mana. Tokens is different from Aristocrats because making bodies comes first, from Counters because it grows wide instead of tall, and from Voltron because it spreads pressure across many creatures. The best token decks EDH players build reward patience against sweepers and careful timing on the finishing turn.
Best Colors for Tokens Decks
Selesnya pairs white generators and anthems with green doublers and overrun finishers.
Orzhov blends tokens with aristocrats payoffs, draining opponents as bodies enter and die.
Gruul makes hasty tokens and converts a fast board into combat pressure.
Izzet creates tokens from spell casts and copies, linking swarms to spellslinger turns.
How Do Tokens Decks Win in Commander?
Tokens decks excel through a specific playstyle and win conditions. Here's how they work:
✓ You deploy repeatable token generators early to establish a wide board.
✓ You convert spells attacks and triggers into extra bodies through doubling effects.
✓ You grow the swarm with anthems and overrun effects when attacks matter.
✓ You hold back a generator so a board wipe does not end your game.
✓ You close by going wide or by converting tokens into damage mana or cards.
Best Cards for Tokens EDH Decks
Doubling Season
Doubles token creation so repeatable generators scale faster.
Anointed Procession
White token doubler that supports go-wide boards at four mana.
Parallel Lives
Green token doubler that stacks with other token multipliers.
Craterhoof Behemoth
Converts a wide board into a lethal trample attack.
Skullclamp
Turns small expendable tokens into repeatable card draw.
How to Build a Tokens Commander Deck
Core Components
✓Run 10-14 repeatable token generators, favoring permanents like Tendershoot Dryad over one-shot spells
✓Include 2-4 token doublers such as Anointed Procession, Parallel Lives, or Mondrak, Glory Dominus
✓Add 4-6 anthem or buff effects so small tokens pressure life totals
✓Use Skullclamp plus 6-8 draw engines that reward having many creatures
✓Play 8-10 ramp pieces because generators and finishers need mana
Win Conditions
✓Use overrun effects like Craterhoof Behemoth or Overwhelming Stampede for one large attack
✓Play drain payoffs such as Impact Tremors or Purphoros, God of the Forge to skip combat
✓Add sacrifice payoffs that convert the swarm into damage or card advantage
✓Use extra combat spells after anthem effects make the board dangerous
Protecting the Swarm
✓Run 3-5 board wipe answers like Heroic Intervention, Flawless Maneuver, or Selfless Spirit
✓Prefer generators that survive sweepers, such as planeswalkers, enchantments, and lands
✓Hold one generator in hand to rebuild after a sweeper
✓Avoid committing every payoff into open mana from control-heavy opponents
Weaknesses of Tokens Decks
⚠Token decks can struggle against repeated board wipes because one sweeper can erase many turns of development.
⚠Individual tokens are small, so large blockers or Propaganda effects can stall attacks.
⚠The strategy leans on doublers and anthems, and targeted removal of those pieces slows the clock.
⚠Going wide is visible, so the table may aim removal at you before the finishing turn.
Is Tokens Right for You?
Tokens is friendly for newer Commander players because the board state shows your progress clearly and many generators are inexpensive. The main skill test is learning when to hold back against sweepers instead of committing every card. It suits players who enjoy building a board and choosing the right turn to make many creatures matter.
Related Commander Strategies
Aristocrats
Tokens makes the bodies; Aristocrats sacrifices them for death triggers and drain.
Sacrifice
Sacrifice uses tokens as fodder, while Tokens values the swarm itself.
+1/+1 Counters
Counters grows creatures taller over time; Tokens spreads power across many bodies.
Aggro
Aggro attacks early with pressure; Tokens builds an engine before the big attack.
Blink
Blink reuses token makers, but it values enter triggers more than the bodies.
Proliferate
Proliferate multiplies counters on tokens, adding growth to a wide board.

Frequently Asked Questions
What makes a good Tokens commander?
The best tokens commanders typically provide consistent access to the strategy's core mechanics. Look for commanders that you deploy repeatable token generators early to establish a wide board.
How do Tokens decks win?
Tokens decks win through a combination of Doubling Season, Anointed Procession, Parallel Lives, and other synergistic pieces that token decks are for players who want one card to become many bodies and then decide whether those bodies attack, block, draw cards, or fuel another engine.
What are the best colors for Tokens?
Selesnya pairs white generators and anthems with green doublers and overrun finishers. However, Orzhov blends tokens with aristocrats payoffs, draining opponents as bodies enter and die.
What are the weaknesses of Tokens decks?
Token decks can struggle against repeated board wipes because one sweeper can erase many turns of development. Individual tokens are small, so large blockers or Propaganda effects can stall attacks. The strategy leans on doublers and anthems, and targeted removal of those pieces slows the clock. Going wide is visible, so the table may aim removal at you before the finishing turn.
