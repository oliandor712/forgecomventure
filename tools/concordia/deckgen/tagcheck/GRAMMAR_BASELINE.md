## Review of the baseline (Claude 2, 2026-10-23, after the scoring tolerances and a parser fix)
Two things changed since the first version of this table (strict V1 61.5%, V3 73.6%), both reasons the first V3 was too low:
1. **Scoring tolerances** (`SCORING_TOLERANCE.md`): `this creature` = name = `~`, `whenever` = `when`, `return ... to the battlefield` = `put ... onto the battlefield`. Effect: accuracy 0.930 -> 0.931 (the scorer was already blind to when / whenever; 'return' vs 'put' and 'this creature' cost a little on a few cards), V1 61.5% -> 62.1% (V1t), V3 73.6% -> 74.7%.
2. **Parser defects found by reading the disagreements** (V3 is a structure measure, so wording and layout differences must not count): the trigger clause was cut after punctuation had been removed (so the whole sentence became the 'trigger'); keywords on one line ("Flash, deathtouch") counted differently from keywords on several lines; one effect split into two sentences ("Draw a card. / Scry N.") counted as two abilities; `{a player}` placeholders were not words. After fixing these V3 is **87.9%** (42 of 348 cards still differ). This is a repair of the measure, not a better reader.

Where the 42 remaining V3 failures come from (read, not counted):
- **Order of abilities:** a reader puts a static line before or after a triggered line (Garruk's Uprising, Yavimaya Elder). A real difference; the grammar must fix the order (keywords, then printed order).
- **Sentence splitting inside a triggered ability** ("When ~ enters, draw a card and scry N." vs "...draw a card. / Scry N."): the reader puts " / " between sentences of one ability. The grammar must say that " / " separates abilities only.
- **Scope and wording of the trigger event:** "attacks enchanted player" vs "enchanted player is attacked" (Curse of Opulence); "you control" before or after the event (Garruk's Packleader); a tag-driven gap, not a style gap.
- **Placeholders** such as `{Trigger}: Create a Treasure token` read as an activated ability by one reader (Nuka-Cola Vending Machine): the grammar needs one spelling for unknown parts.
- **Modes** ("Choose one -" on the first line vs the same line): layout.

Measures that need no repair: V2 text agreement 0.984, V4 score spread 0.008, accuracy 0.932. S1b (61%) is only the share of readers writing `~`; under the new tolerance `this creature` is not an error for accuracy or V-metrics.

What the baseline says to the phrase book (revised): the biggest wording differences left are not return / put or when / whenever (now tolerated) but (1) `under your control` / `under its owner's control` presence (controller tag), (2) X definitions and "equal to its power" wording, (3) placeholder spelling, (4) ability-word conditions, (5) scope on `restrict`, (6) ability order and the meaning of " / ".
Unverified: parser still a first version; V3 compares concept sets without scope words (creature, target, you control, opponent, may), so scope disagreements are not in V3 by design (they are in the accuracy score).
