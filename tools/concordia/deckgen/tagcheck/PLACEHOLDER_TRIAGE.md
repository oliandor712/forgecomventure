## Findings (written by the main session from the data and a reading of the examples)
1. **317 occurrences on 88 cards** in C2. By placeholder: `{cost}` 117, `{effect}` 92, `{filter}` 86, `{trigger}` 16, `{restriction}` 6.
2. **`{cost}` (111 of 117) is the known structural gap**: mana amounts are not tags, so every reader must write a placeholder. Readers agree on it, so it costs little: rough exact-agreement (simple normaliser, not the official V1) is 78.6% on 28 cards whose only placeholder is `{cost}`, against 82.8% on 250 cards with no placeholder. Fix is not a reader fix: either add a cost-amount tag (for example `cost_mana:2B`) or accept the placeholder in the target.
3. **`{effect}` and `{filter}` (178 occurrences) are the real cost.** On the 70 cards with a placeholder other than `{cost}`, rough exact agreement is only 58.6% (41 of 70). Fixing these toward 83% would lift V1 by about 5 points.
4. **Biggest pattern: a trigger without its triggering card type.** `trigger:etb` or `trigger:dies` with `trigger_scope:any/other` and no `trigger_filter:<type>` produces `another {filter}` (Blood Artist, Decoction Module, Garruk's Packleader, Welcoming Vampire; the real text says creature). 11 cards on `{filter}`, 7 on `{effect}` with `trigger_filter` only. Likely fix: always emit `trigger_filter:<cardtype>` for the triggering object (tag fix, needs approval), or a grammar default "an unfiltered trigger object is `creature`" (grammar fix, but wrong for non-creature triggers).
5. **Polarity gap**: Dragon's Rage Channeler has `trigger_filter:card trigger_filter:creature` and the real text is "noncreature spell". The tag cannot say "non". Needs a negation form (tag fix).
6. **Derived amounts**: Creeping Bloodsucker, Gray Merchant, Malakir Blood-Priest, Syr Konrad (`{effect}` with `target_scope` only): "gain life equal to the damage / life lost this way" has no tag. Needs a tag for "amount equals the result of the previous effect".
7. **No-tag groups** (`no_tags`: 13 `{effect}`, 9 `{filter}`, 4 `{trigger}`): the group contains only scaffold tags, so the tags carry nothing (Desecrate Reality, Herald's Horn, Hunted Wumpus, Scorch Spitter, Loyal Apprentice, Nazar). These are genuine missing tags or unsupported scripts; check each against the card script before proposing a tag.
8. **Class `has_tags` does not mean grammar issue.** In most examples read the tags are present but incomplete (items 4 to 6), so treat `has_tags` as "tags insufficient or misread" until each is checked.
