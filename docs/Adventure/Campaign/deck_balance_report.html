Concordia Deck Balance

Five tiers of 20 AI decks each, tested against their own tier at equal life, with one round of revisions.

Each deck played 10 AI-vs-AI games against 10 different decks of its tier, both sides at the tier's standard life (11, 18, 28, 35 and 40 for tiers 1-5). A balanced deck wins between 30% and 70%. The 16 decks outside that band were rebuilt once, then each played 6 more games against unchanged decks of its tier. With 6 to 10 games, a single result swings about ±15-20%, so treat individual numbers as a direction, not a verdict.

Revisions

Decks at 80% or more were rebuilt with a lower power target; decks at 20% or less with a higher one (tier 1: allowed stronger commons). Decks built from a Forge deck also had that deck's pull reduced so the target could steer them, and two tier 2 decks dropped a weak theme.

Still to fix

  • Tomik's Vanguard (tier 4) won all 6 games after being made weaker. Its strength is the commander's plan, not card power, so the power target doesn't reach it. It needs a hand edit or a theme change.
  • Damia (tier 4) and Muzzio (tier 3) stayed weak (0 and 1 of 6). Damia rose in power and still loses, which points at a deck plan the AI plays poorly, not at card quality. Moritte and Medomai only just reached the band (2 of 6).
  • Mirri, Lyra and Sheoldred still won 5 of 6 after being made weaker, and Yahenni and Dalakos overshot to 5 of 6 after being made stronger. All are within the noise of 6 games; a longer run (20 games per deck) would settle them.
  • Card pool: above tier 1, the builder only uses cards that appear in at least one of Forge's shipped decks (17,338 of 26,974 eligible cards). Opening the rest would add variety at the cost of more untested cards.

How this was run

  • Decks: tools/concordia/deckgen/decks.json, built by build.py into forge-gui/res/adventure/Concordia/decks/tier1-tier5. Tiers 2-5 were built down from Forge's Realm of Legends and Commander precon decks; tier 1 from each commander's creature types.
  • Games: Forge's headless simulator, Forge AI on both sides, 150-second limit per game (no game hit it). 500 games in the first run, 96 in the confirmation run.
  • Revisions use per-deck settings in decks.json: target (power target), weakness (tier 1), seed_bonus (how strongly the source deck's cards are kept).
