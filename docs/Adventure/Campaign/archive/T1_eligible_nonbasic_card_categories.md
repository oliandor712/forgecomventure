# Tier 1 Eligible Nonbasic Card Categories

14,057 nonland cards are tier 1 eligible; 37 ability categories reach the 25-card minimum, and 6 fall below it.

Eligible means: printed at common or uncommon (tier 1 allows no rares or mythics), power at that print at or below 25.4 (the 95th percentile of cards in today's tier 1 decks; tier 1 decks average at most 19), and none of: Game Changer, mass land denial, extra turns, fast mana, tutor, board wipe (tier 1 allows zero of each). Lands are excluded entirely; the 25-card cutoff counts cards, not decks.

Categories come from card scripts and Oracle text in the pool (28,948 cards, rebuilt 2026-10-21). Removal, ramp, draw and counterspell use the deck tool's script tags; the rest are Oracle-text patterns, so counts are approximate. One card can sit in several categories, so counts overlap. Colour columns: W U B R G, M = multicolour, C = colourless.

Tier 1 rules these categories sit under (from the deck tool): ramp 0-3 cards, draw 0-3, removal 0-3, no board wipes, no tutors, no fast mana, at least 26 creatures, 40-46 lands. Today's 68 tier 1 aggro decks and 12 token decks have no ramp or control group, which is what these notes are meant to settle.
