# Tier 1 Most-Used Ability Cards

Fifty ability cards appear in 14 or more of the 80 tier 1 decks; only 14 are used across 4–5 colours, and all of those are artifacts.

Picked by: distinct tier 1 decks that hold the card, then number of colours of those decks. Excluded: lands, and cards with no abilities (stat sticks). The list was capped at 50; four cards tied at 14 decks were cut alphabetically (Threaten, Treason of Isengard, Treasure Hunt, Wash Out).

The Reading column is terse TOON-style (effect, cost, trigger, scale) and is my understanding of the card, taken from its Oracle text. Correct it where I misread, and use Your notes for the rule or logic you want implemented in deck building or AI play.

## Cross-colour cards (14 artifacts, 4–5 colours)

Mana and equipment dominate: Arcane Signet is in all 80 decks and Nuka-Cola Vending Machine in 74, which makes tier 1 mana-fixing and food/treasure generation the most common ability on the plane.
