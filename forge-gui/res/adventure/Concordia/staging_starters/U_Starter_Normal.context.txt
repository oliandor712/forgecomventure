Candidate 99-card Commander starter deck. Declared archetypes: tokens and spellslinger. Commander profile: attack, scry, spells, token, tribe:Bird. The commander is one of the cards in the list. Judge every card against the archetypes and the deck_rules, with the whole deck in mind. deck_rules (how this deck was built; judge cards by these, do not reject a card for following them): (1) Power aim is about 27 on the card-power scale, a mid-power deck; do not call solid playables weak. (2) Budget staples are intentional: cheap mana rocks (about 2-3) and nonbasic lands (about 5, picked last) that many precons and Archidekt decks run, any rarity. (3) Commander support: at least 5 cards must work the commander ability itself; those are on plan even if they are off the declared archetypes. (4) A commander that makes tokens needs setup (token makers, fodder, generators; 7 to 14) and payoffs (sacrifice outlets, death triggers, anthems, doublers, go-wide finishers; 5 to 10, at most setup + 3); both are on plan. (5) Utility roles (ramp, card draw, removal, board wipes, recursion, protection) are quota cards and are on plan; only a card with no role, no archetype link and no commander link is a reject. (6) Tribal or theme cards are allowed only when the deck has enough other members of that type; flag any that lack them. (7) Colourless spells are capped by colourless sources; no mass land denial; few off-plan artifacts. (8) Rarity is not restricted.
